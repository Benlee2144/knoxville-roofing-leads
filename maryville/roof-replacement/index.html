A new roof in Maryville might go on a 1920s bungalow a few blocks from Maryville College or on a newer house out toward the foothills, and quotes for those two jobs can look very different. Two local facts shape both: the city runs its own codes office, where the 2024 International Residential Code took effect January 1, 2026, and Blount County sits outside Tennessee's Home Improvement license program. Here is how to plan a replacement around Maryville's housing, weather and paperwork.

What a tear-off can uncover on older Maryville houses

The National Register nomination for the Indiana Avenue Historic District traces houses from the 1880s through the 1930s, including the towered Queen Anne Barnes House at 116 Indiana Avenue, designed by Knoxville mail-order architect George Barber, and bungalows built from local and mail-order plans, one of them supplied by Sears and Roebuck. Most roofs were recorded as composition shingles, though the survey also describes a hipped standing-seam metal roof and a gable roof of pressed metal shingles.

Towers, dormers and porch roofs take hand work, so a replacement quote for a house like this should list each one instead of folding them into a single square-foot price. Ask whether original metal will be repaired, replaced or covered, and get a per-sheet price for new decking. The survey also counted forty-two historic sheds and garages in the district; if yours has a worn roof, pricing it with the house saves a second mobilization.

Historic review, permits and licensing

Maryville has local historic districts, including College Hill and Oak Park, where the Maryville Historic Zoning Commission reviews exterior work. Roofs are part of that: the commission's May 13, 2024 agenda included a roof replacement request for a house in the Oak Park district. If your home is in a local district, ask Development Services about review before you settle on a material or color.

Inside city limits, building permits and inspections run through the Codes office in Maryville's Development Services Department at 416 W. Broadway Avenue. Homes outside the city go to Blount County Development Services, which lists the 2018 International Code Council codes. Because Blount did not adopt the state Home Improvement law, there is no $3,000-to-$24,999 license tier here, but a replacement priced at $25,000 or more requires a state contractor's license, and the roofing classification is BC-21.

Choosing materials for foothill wind and weather

The National Weather Service's Knoxville climate records come from McGhee Tyson Airport, which sits in Alcoa on Maryville's northern edge. Those records show about 52 inches of precipitation in a normal year and an average of 68 days with a low at or below freezing, so underlayment, flashing and attic ventilation deserve as much attention in a quote as the shingle itself.

Wind deserves its own line in the contract. Mountain-wave windstorms off the Smokies are most common from November through March, and on March 30, 2022 the airport sensor near Alcoa recorded a 64 mph gust. Ask for the shingle's wind rating and nailing pattern in writing. For budgeting, 2026 cost guides put architectural shingles at about $5.00 to $10.50 per square foot installed in Tennessee and standing-seam metal, a material already found on some historic Maryville roofs, at roughly $10 to $16.

Before you sign: checklist

  • Confirm whether your address is in Maryville city limits or unincorporated Blount County
  • In College Hill, Oak Park or another local district, ask Development Services about review first
  • Get the shingle's wind rating and nailing pattern written into the contract
  • Have towers, dormers, porch roofs and garages priced as separate line items
  • Get a per-sheet price for replacing rotted decking
  • Look up any state license and ask for proof of liability and workers' comp coverage
  • Keep the deposit to one-third of the contract price or less

COMMON QUESTIONS

Roof Replacement in Maryville: Questions

Does replacing a roof in Maryville need a city permit?

Ask before work starts. The Codes office in Maryville's Development Services Department handles building permits and inspections inside city limits, and the department's main number is (865) 273-3500. The city's posted pages don't spell out re-roofing rules, so have your roofer confirm with that office and write into the contract who pulls any permit that is required.

I live in the College Hill area. Can I switch to metal or change the roof color?

Possibly, but check first. College Hill is one of Maryville's local historic districts, and the Maryville Historic Zoning Commission reviews exterior projects there; roof replacements have appeared on its agendas. Calling Development Services before you sign keeps an approval question from stalling a job that is already on a roofer's calendar.

What does a full roof replacement cost in Maryville?

The 2026 cost guides used on this site don't break out Maryville separately. Modernize's Knoxville-area range for a 2,000 square foot architectural-shingle roof is about $11,500 to $20,000, and a Tennessee metal roof of that size runs about $18,000 to $36,000. Steep, cut-up rooflines on older houses add labor, so treat these as rough ranges and rely on written quotes.
