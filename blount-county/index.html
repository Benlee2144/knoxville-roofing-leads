Blount County runs from Fort Loudoun Lake on its northern edge to Thunderhead Mountain, its highest point at 5,527 feet, inside Great Smoky Mountains National Park. Chilhowee Mountain crosses the middle of the county and marks the geologic line between the Ridge-and-Valley and the Blue Ridge. That split shapes roofing here: suburban streets around Maryville and Alcoa on one side, and coves, cabins and steep wooded lots around Townsend, Walland and Happy Valley on the other.

Mountain-wave winds along the foothills

The western foothills of the Smokies get a particular kind of windstorm. When strong winds cross the mountains, air can race down the slopes in what forecasters call a mountain wave, and NWS Morristown research finds these events most often from November through March. During the March 30, 2022 event, a park wind tower at Look Rock on Chilhowee Mountain measured a 77 mph gust, and the airport sensor near Alcoa recorded 64 mph.

Gusts like that tend to start at roof edges, rakes and ridge caps. For a foothill home, it is worth asking a roofer about wind-rated shingles, the fastening pattern at the edges, and whether metal makes sense for your pitch and exposure.

Cabins in Townsend and Walland

Townsend sits in Tuckaleechee Cove at about 1,076 feet and calls itself the Peaceful Side of the Smokies, with U.S. 321, the Lamar Alexander Parkway, linking it to Walland and Maryville. The town grew from the Little River Lumber Company, founded in 1901, and tourism has kept the cove busy since the national park opened. Cabins set against wooded slopes are a common sight.

Cabin roofs raise their own questions: heavy shade that keeps shingles damp, leaves packing into valleys, steep or narrow drives that limit where a dumpster can sit, and, for rentals, timing work between guest stays. Bring those details up when roofers call.

Lake towns and the western county

Along the northern edge, Fort Loudoun Lake forms Louisville's border. The town was a flatboat and steamboat port when it incorporated in 1851 and has grown quickly in recent years. Friendsville, founded by Quakers from North Carolina in the 1790s, stretches from the lake south toward U.S. 321, with farms and newer homes along Miser Station Road.

For permits, Blount County Development Services enforces the 2018 edition of the International Code Council's residential and commercial codes and takes applications for construction permits. If you live inside Maryville, Alcoa or another city, have your roofer confirm which office handles your address.

COMMON QUESTIONS

Roofing Questions from Blount County Homeowners

Why do roofs near Walland and Happy Valley take such a beating from wind?

These communities sit in the western foothills of the Smokies, where mountain-wave winds can roar downslope, mostly between November and March. On March 30, 2022, a park wind tower at Look Rock recorded a 77 mph gust. A roofer should check edges, ridge caps and fastening, since wind usually gets a grip at those points first.

What building code applies in Blount County?

Blount County Development Services says it enforces the commercial and residential regulations of the 2018 edition of the International Code Council through plan review and inspections, and it accepts applications for construction permits. If your home is inside a city such as Maryville or Alcoa, ask your roofer to confirm with the correct office whether a permit is needed.

Can roofers get materials up to a mountain cabin outside Townsend?

Usually, but access shapes the quote. Narrow gravel drives, switchbacks and tight turnarounds may mean smaller trucks, hand-carrying bundles or a slower tear-off. Send photos of the driveway and roof when roofers contact you, and mention whether the cabin is rented so the work can be planned between stays.
