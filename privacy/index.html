Privacy Policy

Effective 2026-10-14

Who we are

Knoxville Roofing Quotes (knoxvilleroofingquotes.com) is a free referral service that connects people who need roofing work with independent roofing contractors in East Tennessee. This policy explains what we collect, how we use it and the choices you have.

Information you give us

When you request quotes we collect the details you enter: the type of project, ZIP code, timeframe, whether you own the property, your name, phone number and email address, and your consent to be contacted. If you use the contact form we collect your name, email address and message.

Information collected automatically

We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use the site (for example, pages viewed, device type and approximate location). Google Analytics uses cookies. You can opt out with the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on.

Advertising

Some pages show ads served by Google AdSense. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visits to this site and other sites on the internet. You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings or www.aboutads.info.

How we use your information

  • To match your request with up to 3 roofing contractors who serve your area and pass your details to them so they can contact you about your project.
  • To reply to messages you send us.
  • To keep the site working, prevent spam and improve our service.

Who we share it with

We share quote requests only with the contractors matched to your project, and with service providers that help us run the site (for example, Formspree, which processes our forms, and Google, for analytics and ads). We do not sell your personal information to marketing lists. We may disclose information if required by law.

Calls and texts

If you consent, contractors may contact you by phone, text or email about your project. Message and data rates may apply. You can reply STOP to any text to opt out, or tell the contractor you no longer wish to be contacted.

Your choices

You can ask us to access, correct or delete the information you submitted, or to stop sharing it, by writing to us through our contact page. Contractors who already received your request keep their own records under their own policies.

Data retention and security

We keep quote requests only as long as needed to provide the service and for our records, and we use reasonable safeguards. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure.

Children

This site is meant for adults arranging work on property. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes

We may update this policy. The effective date at the top shows when it last changed.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Call or text Ben Arp at (423) 920-4333, or reach us through our contact page.
