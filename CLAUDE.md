# Knoxville Roofing Leads - Project Notes

Static site for knoxvilleroofingquotes.com, a free roofing-quote referral service (not a roofer). Hosted on GitHub Pages from `main`.

## How pages are built

Most pages are generated. After any change to templates, data, `styles.css` or `script.js`, run:

```
python3 _build/build.py
```

- `_build/build.py` renders city/county pages, service pages, blog posts, the blog index and service-areas hub, refreshes the homepage's shared blocks, and writes `sitemap.xml` and `zips.json`. It also stamps a content hash onto every `styles.css?v=` / `script.js?v=` link so browsers never mix old CSS with new HTML.
- `_build/pages.py` holds one-off pages: cost calculator, quote worksheet, how it works, about, privacy, terms, contact, 404, thank-you.
- Data lives in `_build/data/`: `existing.json`, `cities_a.json`, `cities_b.json`, `counties.json` (locations), `services.json`, `town_services.json` (town + service pages such as `/karns/roof-replacement/`, rendered by `town_service_page()`; a service only gets a `/{slug}/` link once it exists in `services.json`), `blog.json` (post metadata), `facts.json` (sourced Tennessee licensing/insurance/cost facts; `site_costs` feeds the calculator and cost tables).
- Blog article bodies live in `_build/blog/<slug>.html`. To add a post: add the body file and an entry in `blog.json` (title up to 60 characters, description 120-160), then build. When you substantially update a post, set `modified` / `modified_label` in its `blog.json` entry. Posts end with a `<ul class="sources">` list of the sources they cite.
- `index.html` is hand-written. Content between `<!-- shared:NAME -->` and `<!-- /shared:NAME -->` markers is overwritten by the build; edit everything else directly.
- GitHub Pages doesn't publish folders starting with `_`, so `_build/` stays private.
- After a deploy goes live, `python3 _build/indexnow.py` pings IndexNow (Bing and others) with every sitemap URL. The key file `a2eeeb20ea5663746f8fbba66eb45ac5.txt` at the site root must stay in place.

## Google Analytics + AdSense

- GA (`G-RPJS68684E`) and the AdSense verification meta tag are on every page (handled by `head()` in build.py).
- The AdSense ad script runs on blog posts only, and not on the high-intent posts listed in `NO_ADS` in build.py. Keep ads off the homepage, location, service and tool pages so they don't send visitors to competitors.
- Lead events: `form_start`, `generate_lead` (fired on /thank-you/), `calculator_used`.

## Lead form

- Posts to Formspree `https://formspree.io/f/xdeawzlj` (free plan: 50 submissions/month). The form markup is generated by `lead_form()` in build.py; don't hand-edit it in pages.
- `script.js` turns it into 3 steps, submits via fetch, and redirects to `/thank-you/`. Service pages preselect the project type and open on step 2.

## Roofer map (/roofers/)

- Data: `_build/data/roofers.json` (name, website, phone, city, county, services). Researched from each company's own website; never copy Google/Yelp/BBB ratings or reviews. Town coordinates come from `_build/data/tn_places.json` (US Census gazetteer); pins sit on the town, not the street address.
- `_build/roofers.py` builds the map page, `/roofers/get-listed/` (badge + add/update/remove form) and the "roofers in/near X" sections on location pages. Map JS: `roofer-map.js`; Leaflet + markercluster are self-hosted in `vendor/`.
- Outbound roofer links stay `rel="nofollow"`. Don't offer followed links in exchange for backlinks (Google treats that as a link scheme); the badge is free with no conditions.

## Theme

- Tennessee orange (#FF8200), white and charcoal with checkerboard trim, Oswald headings, football touches (form steps are "downs", thank-you page says "Touchdown!"). Colors live in the `:root` variables at the top of styles.css.
- Orange text on white must use `--orange-ink` (small text) or `--orange-strong` (large headings) for contrast; bright `--orange` is for fills, buttons (with dark text) and dark backgrounds.
- Don't use University of Tennessee marks or names (Power T, Vols/Volunteers, Smokey, Rocky Top, Big Orange, Neyland) or imply affiliation.

## Content rules

- Use the inline SVG icons (`icon()` in build.py), never emojis.
- No made-up stats, ratings, review counts, savings percentages or testimonials. Only add real, permitted reviews and photos.
- The site is a referral service: never imply it does roofing work. Photos of real jobs need the roofer's permission and a credit line; AI or stock images must be captioned as illustrations.
- Local facts and legal/cost claims must be sourced (see `facts.json`). No permit fees or rules unless confirmed from an official source.
