BEFORE YOU HIRE

What to Require From Any Roofer

Whether you use our service or not, these four checks protect you from most roofing problems

A Tennessee contractor license

Roofing jobs of $25,000 or more need a state contractor's license, and in Knox County home jobs from $3,000 up need a Home Improvement license. Look roofers up at verify.tn.gov.

How to check a license

Proof of insurance

Ask for a certificate of liability insurance (and workers' comp if they have employees) sent directly from their insurance agent.

A written, itemized quote

Materials, tear-off, decking repairs, ventilation, cleanup and warranty should all be on paper, so you can compare quotes fairly.

No big deposit up front

Tennessee limits home improvement deposits to one-third of the price. Be wary of storm door-knockers who push you to sign or pay on the spot.

AFTER A STORM

Storm Damage? Do This First

  1. Stay safe. Stay off the roof and away from downed lines or hanging limbs.
  2. Limit water damage. Move valuables, catch drips, and get a tarp on the roof if it can be done safely. How to tarp a roof.
  3. Document everything. Take dated photos of the roof, gutters, siding and any interior damage.
  4. Get a licensed roofer to inspect. A written damage report helps you decide whether a claim makes sense.
  5. Call your insurer and keep notes. Write down claim numbers, names and dates. Our insurance claim guide.

COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a new roof cost in Knoxville, TN?

Most Knoxville-area roof replacements fall somewhere between $8,000 and $25,000, depending on the size of the home, the material and how complex the roof is. Asphalt shingles are the most affordable option, while metal costs more but lasts longer. Try our roof cost calculator for a quick estimate, then compare written quotes.

Is this roofing quote service really free?

Yes. The service is 100% free for homeowners. We connect you with up to 3 licensed, insured roofing contractors who provide free estimates, and there is no obligation to hire anyone. Contractors pay us a fee for the introduction.

How quickly will I hear from roofers?

Roofers usually reach out within 24-48 hours of your request. For emergencies like an active leak or storm damage, choose "ASAP / emergency" in the form so they know it is urgent.

How do I know if I need a roof repair or a full replacement?

A few missing shingles, a small leak or localized damage can usually be repaired. A roof that is 20 or more years old, has widespread damage, sagging, multiple leaks or daylight showing through the decking usually needs replacing. Roofers can inspect and tell you which makes sense.

Will my homeowners insurance cover roof damage?

Most policies cover damage from sudden events such as hail, wind, fallen trees or fire, but not normal wear and tear or lack of maintenance. Document the damage with dated photos and have a roofer inspect it before or while you file a claim.

What roofing materials are best for Tennessee weather?

Architectural asphalt shingles are the most popular choice in East Tennessee because they balance cost and durability. Metal roofing is growing in popularity for its long lifespan and energy efficiency, especially on cabins and rural homes. Flat commercial roofs usually use TPO or EPDM.

How do I check that a roofer is licensed in Tennessee?

Search the contractor on the State of Tennessee license lookup at verify.tn.gov. Roofing jobs of $25,000 or more require a state contractor license, and in Knox County residential jobs from $3,000 to $24,999 require a state Home Improvement license. Also ask for proof of liability insurance sent directly from their agent.
