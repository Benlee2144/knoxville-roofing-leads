How It Works

A free, no-pressure way for East Tennessee homeowners to compare roofing quotes.

What Knoxville Roofing Quotes is

Knoxville Roofing Quotes is a free referral service for homeowners and property owners in Knoxville and the surrounding East Tennessee counties. We are not a roofing company and we don't send crews. We pass your request to up to 3 independent local roofing contractors who handle your type of project, so you can compare quotes without calling around.

How it works

  1. You tell us about the job. Pick the type of project, enter your ZIP code and how soon you need it, and leave your contact details.
  2. We match you with up to 3 roofers. We look for contractors who work in your area and do your kind of work: storm damage, replacement, repair, metal or commercial.
  3. They contact you to set up an estimate. Roofers usually reach out within 24-48 hours to schedule a free inspection.
  4. You compare and decide. Hire whoever earns it, or no one. There is no obligation and no fee to you.

How we make money

Contractors pay us for the introductions. That is why the service is free for you. It also means we have a reason to send your request only to roofers who are a good fit, because a bad match is a waste for everyone.

What we ask of the roofers we work with

We work with contractors who tell us they hold the Tennessee license their work requires and carry liability insurance. We still recommend that you check both yourself before you sign anything.

In Tennessee, roofing jobs of $25,000 or more require a state contractor's license. In Knox County, home roofing jobs from $3,000 to $24,999 also require a state Home Improvement license; neighboring counties are not on that list, so ask your local codes office what applies there. Tennessee also limits home improvement deposits to one-third of the contract price.

  • Look up the contractor's license on the State of Tennessee's verification site: verify.tn.gov.
  • Ask for a certificate of insurance sent directly from their insurance agent.
  • Get every price, material and warranty in writing.
  • Never pay a large deposit to someone who just knocked on your door after a storm.

Your information
